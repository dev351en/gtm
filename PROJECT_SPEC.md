Temporary city destruction/restoration; ordinary wilderness survival. Day 20 real minutes, night 10. Secure offline login, whitelist configurable default off.
- Entertainment: dance clubs, no gambling; fictional alcohol penalties and individual blackout, not global clock changes. Manual-only festivals (8, including Christmas/New Year), reversible city-wide decorations, billboards and clothing. Phone and Car App, authorized music only, news generated from verified game events.
- Hosting: preserve original sandbox. Optional eva_ai smallest permitted configuration, lightweight model only if quota and credit balance verified. No paid upgrades, no production deletion/resizing, no credential disclosure. Billing/actual disk limits unverified. No unattended deploy.
- Publication: portable core, city data separate, licenses checked, reproducible builds, documentation and crossplay matrix; no public marketplace publication without approval.
