# Native auto-rickshaw client acceptance script

**Status:** Pending actual execution. This is a test procedure, not a test result.
**Target:** The exact candidate JAR/resource packs and Paper26.3 build recorded in INTERACTION_RUNTIME_R4_REPORT.md.

Use a disposable copy of the inspected world and records. Retain the original backup. Record the server/JAR/pack hashes, Java client version, Bedrock/Geyser/Floodgate versions, player UUIDs and screenshots/video in the result log. Only the staging owner enables vehicles.enabled; leave all-road-models false for this first auto qualification. Ensure the commissioned gas/maintenance site IDs resolve in this copied world. Do not accept guessed site coordinates as evidence.

Use two authenticated ordinary accounts A and B, plus a separate staging administrator. Both ordinary accounts need the intended gtm.vehicles.use permission. Repeat relevant controls/rendering steps with one actual Java client and one actual Bedrock client, including touch/controller input where available.

| Step | Action | Required result/evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Start with the feature disabled and inspect existing accounts/worlds. | No new road entities or balances. Existing UUIDs and records preserved. |
| 2 | Enable the staging feature, restart cleanly, open phone → Garage → Shop. | Three auto-rickshaw models offered with intended prices and seat/energy descriptions. Additional road profiles remain gated. |
| 3 | Try vehicle GUI/interactions/commands while logged out and while a prior callback is queued. | No purchase, grant, mounting, movement or service; stale session callbacks do nothing. |
| 4 | With insufficient bank funds, confirm a purchase; then fund A legitimately and buy each of the three autos on open inspected ground. | No debit on insufficient funds. Each paid receipt creates exactly one stable owned vehicle; ledger and module records agree. |
| 5 | Repeat a purchase action/double click, reconnect during delivery and retry the same committed grant ID through the controlled test harness. | No repeated debit or second vehicle. No safe parking space leaves a paid intent pending with a visible explanation. |
| 6 | Walk around each model in daylight and at night. Compare against authored dimensions and four state references. | Correct model/atlas, full-size world scale, front orientation, grounded wheels and appropriate culling. Record any Java/Bedrock rendering difference. |
| 7 | A enters the driver seat and B attempts entry while locked. Unlock passenger access, then admit B. Test both rear seats on traditional/electric autos, entry through a solid wall and a deliberately rejected mount callback. | Locked outsider and through-wall entry rejected; unlocked B is a passenger and cannot drive. Rejected mount leaves no rider/seat reservation. Cargo auto exposes only its driver seat. Check hip, head, roof and camera fit. |
| 8 | Drive using forward/reverse, left/right and jump braking; try a GUI while holding throttle. | Both actual rider positions move with the vehicle; reverse first brakes; speed stays bounded; GUI applies braking. No dismount on the first movement tick. |
| 9 | Drive beside a wall and through a diagonal gap. Approach a thin fence, slab/ramp, low ceiling, water, ledge and unloaded boundary. | Body sweep prevents tunnelling. Supported small steps work; unsafe slopes/ledges/water/unloaded space stop safely without loading/generating terrain. |
| 10 | Approach another vehicle, a pedestrian/NPC and owned furniture. Walk into a stationary vehicle on foot. | Vehicle and pedestrian body blocking work; no cosmetic-only pass-through. Nearby furniture blocks the footprint. Distant furniture counts do not disable motion. |
| 11 | Attempt entry into a locked private region, including a small claim inside the body's footprint. Repeat with B unauthorized and A authorized. Revoke B's region access while seated in a parked auto with no driver. | Complete occupied body and every occupant are checked at entry and while parked or moving; passenger access cannot bypass property permissions. Revoked access ends B's occupancy. |
| 12 | Give B a driving key, let B drive, then revoke it. Separately revoke gtm.vehicles.use while B is seated. Test a copied decorative item and another vehicle's ID. | Only the registered key grants driving. Key or runtime-permission revocation stops B; runtime-permission revocation detaches the rider. Items/lore/other IDs cannot acquire ownership. |
| 13 | Sneak to exit while stopped and moving. Block one or all exit sides with real geometry. Try a nearby parked vehicle or person in the exit spot. Use a staging callback to cancel an exit teleport. | Vehicle stops, selects a clear safe exit, and does not place the player into another body. With no safe exit, normal exit is refused with guidance. A canceled voluntary teleport restores the same valid rider where possible and reports that the exit did not complete. |
| 14 | Log out, lose authentication, disconnect, die or teleport while driving/ride as passenger. | Driver stops immediately. No stale nonce controls it. Real teleport/medical destinations win without nested relocation. Reconnect requires authentication and does not restore a stale rider. |
| 15 | Drive until energy decreases; verify odometer against travelled distance. In controlled fixtures, exhaust energy and exercise health thresholds. | No movement beyond remaining funded distance, no negative fuel, damaged/critical/wreck models appear at documented thresholds, failed/wrecked vehicles cannot propel. |
| 16 | At the actual registered gas/maintenance site, open Garage → Services and refill/repair. Try outside the site, while moving, full energy/condition, non-owner and insufficient funds. | New paid work requires every gate; rejected requests take no payment. Valid services change condition and commit their receipt exactly once. |
| 17 | Inject a ledger acknowledgement failure after a service snapshot succeeds; leave the site and reconnect/retry. | The committed service is recognized and acknowledged without another charge/refill. A new unresolved service still requires the real station. |
| 18 | Park, record ID/world/pose/energy/health/odometer/keys, stop cleanly and restart. Move away until entities unload, then return. | State and identity survive; only one component set appears. No duplicate ownership or force-loaded chunks. |
| 19 | On disposable copies only, inject malformed snapshot, unwritable storage and process termination during a checkpoint/grant/service. | Corrupt data is retained and rejected. Failed commits do not publish ownership/service completion. Record actual rewind/cross-file behavior; do not infer power-loss guarantees from ordinary restart. |
| 20 | Exercise the configured active/owner/global limits and multiple simultaneous drivers. Measure tick time, CPU, memory and entity counts. | Limits are enforced without unauthenticated bypass; no silent vehicle deletion; fixed budgets and fair simulation order hold. Record measured performance instead of an assumed TPS claim. |

For each step record **PASS**, **FAIL** or **NOT RUN**, the edition, evidence path and any reproducible defect. Qualification of the three autos does not automatically qualify the other 62 profiles, large-vehicle seats, animation, traffic, towing, boats, rail or aircraft. All unresolved failures remain release gates.
